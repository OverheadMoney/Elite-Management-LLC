The practice

Three disciplines, one operating model.

Every engagement is built from the same three parts. We diagnose where money and time actually go, redesign the structure that produces those results, and stay in the room while the new structure takes hold.

DIAGNOSE

Operating Diagnostic

A 30-day read of the whole business: cash conversion, contract economics, vendor spend, staffing load, and the decisions nobody owns. You get a single document that says what is true and what it costs.

  • Cost-to-serve by customer and contract
  • Vendor and financing-partner review
  • Billing and collections audit
DESIGN

Structural Redesign

We rebuild the parts that leak: pricing tiers, service agreements, vendor terms, the monthly close, and the management rhythm. Each change is modeled before it is signed.

  • Contract and tier architecture
  • Margin and pricing models
  • Financing and capital structure
DELIVER

Embedded Execution

A standing seat at the leadership table for a defined term. We run the cadence, hold the numbers to account, and hand the system back when it runs without us.

  • Fractional operating leadership
  • Weekly metrics and decision log
  • Transition plan at exit

Principles

How we hold ourselves to account.

I

Numbers before narrative.

No recommendation is made until the cost of the current state is measured. Opinion is cheap; a reconciled ledger is not.

II

Every contract is a design decision.

Terms, tiers and renewals shape margin more than any sales push. We treat them as engineered documents.

III

Owners should own less of the work.

The measure of a good structure is how much of the business runs when the owner is not in the building.

IV

Leave a system, not a dependency.

Engagements end on purpose, with a cadence, a metrics stack and a team that carries it forward.
