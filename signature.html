Email signature

Built to match elitemgmt.io. Fill in the name and title, click Copy, and paste into Gmail's signature editor. It renders on both light and dark email clients.

Install in Gmail

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. Gmail → gear → See all settings → General → Signature → Create new, name it "Elite Management".
  3. Click into the editor and paste (Cmd+V). The seal image loads from elitemgmt.io.
  4. Under Signature defaults, choose it for new emails and replies on the value@elitemgmt.io send-as address. Save.
